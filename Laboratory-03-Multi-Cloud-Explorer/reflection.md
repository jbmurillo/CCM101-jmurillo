# Cloud Platforms Reflection

Among the three cloud platforms, Azure impressed me the most. I found it easier to understand during my research because it offers many services for computing, storage, databases, and other cloud needs. I also liked that it works well with Microsoft products.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similar features. All three offer services like virtual machines, storage, databases, networking, and security. They also let users choose the resources they need and increase or decrease them when needed.

Business needs are important when choosing a cloud platform. A company should consider its budget, applications, storage needs, security, and number of users. For example, a company that already uses Microsoft products may choose Azure, while a company that focuses on AI may choose Google Cloud.

Being a Cloud Solutions Consultant helped me understand that choosing a cloud provider is not only about choosing the most popular one. I learned that I should first look at the needs of the client. Comparing AWS, Azure, and GCP also helped me see that similar services can have different features and advantages.

I also became more comfortable making Markdown files and organizing my work in GitHub. Overall, this activity helped me understand cloud platforms better and gave me more experience in documenting technical work.
